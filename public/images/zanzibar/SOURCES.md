# Zanzibar excursion photographs

From Wikimedia Commons, downloaded on 25 September 2026 and cropped to 3:2 to
match the itinerary photographs. They illustrate the excursion; none of them is
a photograph of an Atlas departure, guide, or guest.

The page renders no credit line under these images: Atlas decided on
25 September 2026 to drop the on-page credits. Eleven of the fifteen are
CC BY or CC BY-SA, whose terms ask for the photographer's name and the licence
wherever the image is shown, so this file is the only remaining record of who
took them. Keep it with the images, and follow the Commons link for the full
terms of each. `src/data/zanzibar-photos.ts` maps a photograph to its
excursion and carries nothing else.
